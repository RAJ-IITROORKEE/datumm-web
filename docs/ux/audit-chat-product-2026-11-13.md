### Live concept-planning recovery gap

The parent tested a live Plan task: a grouped footprint and entrance question accepted “10 m wide by 8 m deep” and South, after which the service requested width and depth separately. Following 10 and 8, the lifecycle displayed **Completed**, while the actual answer said the confirmed brief was saved, planning stopped because confirmed questions repeated, and no plan was generated. The Design area remained empty; the offered next prompts were generic explanation/BIM checklist prompts, with no specific recovery action. This is an observed task result, not a claim that all planning attempts fail. An explicit amend-brief follow-up is being tested separately by the parent.

`src/components/workspace/conversation.tsx:499–502, 543–548, 582` drives the completed process and fallback suggestions from lifecycle and design presence. `src/lib/process-activity.mjs:36–39` maps a `completed` lifecycle to “Completed”; `conversation-answer.tsx:23–25, 39–43` enables next prompts for a saved current completed answer. This describes a completed turn, but in this case does not distinguish completion of the response from completion of the requested plan. The answer itself is honest; the surrounding status and generic actions fail to help the user recover. `src/lib/workflow-state.ts:32` supplies checkpoint recovery only when a failure/error or listed stopped lifecycle exists, so that branch does not establish recovery for every completed response that describes a stopped planner.

**UI-only proposal:** When an authoritative structured outcome identifies a stopped/partial plan or missing deliverable, display that outcome alongside turn settlement, offer an appropriate explicit draft-only amend/retry path if the service contract supports it, and suppress unrelated suggestions. Do not infer planner success from `completed`, parse arbitrary answer prose as authority, auto-submit, or imply a drawing exists. Validate against completed Ask, successful Plan, partial and failed cases.

**Backend-dependent:** Preserving confirmed footprint semantics across repeated/versioned question receipts is a service behavior; the documented source/container checks are not evidence that this correction is deployed. Confirm deployment and the exact structured failure/recovery contract before tying a UI action to it. No backend change or deployment was made in this audit.
