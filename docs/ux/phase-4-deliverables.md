## Scope and source truth

1. Consistent human-readable concept names and recorded versions across loaded assets, details and preview; technical IDs/fingerprints remain in source disclosure. Ordinals come from unique plan IDs in the supplied candidate order, not invented names or durable naming storage.
2. Distinguish current drawing, retained reference and known previous version. Do not call another concept a previous revision. Comparison offers an explicit previous-version read when version > 1, or an explicit actual alternative; absence/failure of a requested version is not proof of exhaustive history.
3. Comparison loading/error/result belongs to its exact selected task/drawing/pair. A failed comparison retains usable geometry and any previous successful comparison. Known comparator fingerprints are validated; the diff API accepts IDs/versions, so its summary is not claimed as independently fingerprint-verified.
4. Existing alternative-selection mutation remains separate from viewing. If selection is uncertain, preserve reference geometry but keep native/current approval blocked until authoritative reconciliation; no automatic retry of a mutation.
5. Explicit Revise brief entry appends an unsent task-brief amendment through the existing guarded mechanism, preserving text/mode and task/account generation. It never submits, accepts a plan or authorizes Revit. Active/pending-question states remain blocked with explanatory text.
6. Exports distinguish browser-generated white sheets/current 3D viewport from server-requested saved plan/version/style, evidence reports and answer-table CSV. Server exports do not pass a fingerprint; show that verification limit in source detail. Model revision is not saved report revision history. D9 immutable BOQ history stays unavailable.
